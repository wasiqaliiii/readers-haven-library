Membership Plans

Join Readers' Haven and borrow from thousands of titles. Choose the plan that fits you.

Student

PKR 1,000 / year

  • Eligibility: Valid student ID required
  • Borrowing limit: 3 books at a time
  • Loan period: 14 days
  • Late return: PKR 5 per day, per book

Adult

PKR 2,500 / year

  • Eligibility: Ages 18 and above, CNIC required
  • Borrowing limit: 5 books at a time
  • Loan period: 21 days
  • Late return: PKR 10 per day, per book

Family

PKR 4,500 / year

  • Eligibility: Up to 4 family members, one address
  • Borrowing limit: 10 books at a time (shared)
  • Loan period: 21 days
  • Late return: PKR 10 per day, per book

Late-Return Rules

  • Books may be renewed once for the same loan period if no one has reserved them.
  • Late fees are capped at the replacement cost of the book.
  • Borrowing is paused until all overdue items are returned and fees are paid.
  • Lost or damaged books must be replaced or paid for at current market price.
