Our Digital Services

Readers' Haven provides a variety of services to make studying and accessing information easier.

1. Online Catalogue

Explore our collection of books, reference materials, and educational resources by browsing different categories.

Availability: Catalogue information is available 24/7.

How to access: Visit the Collections page to browse available titles.

Browse Collections

2. E-Books & Digital Resources

Discover digital reading resources and information about library membership and learning facilities.

Availability: Digital resource information is accessible 24/7.

How to access: Visit the Membership page to explore membership options.

View Membership

3. Study Room Booking

Our library offers quiet study spaces for individual learning and group discussions.

Timings: Monday to Saturday, 9:00 AM – 6:00 PM.

How to book: Contact the library team with your preferred date and time to request room availability.

Contact Library

4. Printing & Scanning

Access document printing and scanning facilities for assignments, research papers, and other educational materials.

Timings: Monday to Saturday, 9:00 AM – 6:00 PM.

How to use: Visit the library service desk during opening hours.

5. Free Wi-Fi

Stay connected using library Wi-Fi while studying, researching, or completing academic work.

Availability: During library opening hours.

How to connect: Ask the reception desk for Wi-Fi access details.
